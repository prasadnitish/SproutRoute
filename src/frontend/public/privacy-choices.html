User Privacy Choices

Control, delete, or ask about your SproutRoute data.

How to delete local app data, stop shared trip links, request server-side deletion, and manage optional permissions.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Delete local data on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open SproutRoute.
  2. Open Settings from the app.
  3. Choose Delete all local trip data.
  4. Confirm deletion.

This removes saved trips, imported profile data, packing progress, cached weather snapshots, local notification plans, widget snapshots, Spotlight records, and Live Activity state from that device. Deleting local data does not stop trip links you already shared, so stop them first (see below). You can also delete local data by uninstalling the app.

Stop a shared trip link

Open the plan in SproutRoute and choose Stop sharing. On the web, use Stop sharing in the browser where you made the link. The link stops working right away for everyone who has it. If you no longer have the plan, email us the link and we will stop it and delete the stored copy. Links you don't stop expire on their own 7 days after the trip ends.

Request server-side deletion

Email nitish.prasad@gmail.com with the subject line SproutRoute data deletion request. Include the email address you used with SproutRoute, if any, and a short description of what you want deleted.

We may ask for information needed to verify the request. Verification information is used only to process the request. We will respond within applicable legal timelines and will explain if we need to retain limited information for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or dispute handling.

Manage optional permissions

  • Location: manage location permission in iOS Settings. SproutRoute can still plan from a typed destination if location permission is off.
  • Notifications: manage reminders in iOS Settings. Disabling notifications stops local reminders from appearing.
  • Widgets, Spotlight, and Live Activities: remove widgets from the Home Screen, delete local trip data in the app, or uninstall the app to clear local extension data.
  • Analytics and diagnostics: the native iOS app has an optional Share Product Analytics toggle in Settings. It is off by default. If enabled in a configured build, SproutRoute sends only aggregate product events to PostHog through a direct event endpoint; it does not use the PostHog SDK, autocapture, session replay, advertising identifiers, raw prompts, profile JSON, child names, pet names, precise GPS, or custom packing item text. Turn the toggle off to stop future iOS product analytics and reset the local analytics identifier. You can also contact us if you have questions about backend reliability data, Apple App Analytics, crash diagnostics, or web product analytics associated with your use. The web app uses PostHog for page views and feature events; it does not record sessions or screens.

Access, correction, and portability

You can email us to request access to, correction of, or export of server-side personal information associated with you. Most iOS trip data is stored locally on your device, so the app itself is the primary place to view and manage saved trips.

California privacy rights

California residents may have rights to know, access, delete, correct, limit certain uses of sensitive personal information, opt out of sale or sharing, and not be discriminated against for exercising privacy rights. We do not sell personal information and do not knowingly share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can exercise rights by emailing nitish.prasad@gmail.com.
