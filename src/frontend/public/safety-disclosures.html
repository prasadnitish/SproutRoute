Safety and AI Disclosures

Useful planning guidance still needs human review.

SproutRoute combines AI, static rules, maps, weather services, and place data. The result is a planning assistant, not a legal or safety authority.

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Human review required

Before travel, verify any safety-sensitive or legal requirement with official sources. This includes car-seat laws, child passenger safety guidance, pet travel and airline rules, border entry requirements, travel advisories, weather alerts, venue hours, road conditions, and neighborhood safety information.

AI-generated itinerary and packing guidance

AI may help parse your trip request, organize activities, create summaries, and suggest packing categories. AI can make mistakes. It can produce stale, incomplete, duplicated, or contextually poor recommendations. Review every result before relying on it.

Car-seat and child passenger guidance

Car-seat cards are informational and may include source, confidence, status, and human-review expectations. They are not legal advice. Laws can change and can depend on child height, weight, seating position, rental vehicle details, medical exceptions, jurisdiction, and enforcement interpretation. Check current rules with local authorities or a certified child passenger safety technician.

Pet travel guidance

Pet travel cards may summarize airline policies, breed restrictions, cabin and cargo constraints, documents, vaccines, country entry rules, and timeline warnings. Airline and government requirements change frequently. Verify directly with the airline, veterinarian, destination country or state, and accommodation before booking or traveling.

Weather and WeatherKit

Weather forecasts are time-sensitive and may differ between sources. Backend weather helps generate the plan, while iOS may use Apple Weather and WeatherKit for destination forecast refreshes and local reminders. Always check official weather alerts and local conditions before travel or outdoor activities.

Maps, places, and route information

Place details, opening hours, photos, ratings, directions, route links, and Apple Maps handoff can be incomplete or outdated. Verify directly with the venue or map provider before making reservations or travel decisions.

Emergency and professional advice

SproutRoute is not for emergency use. In an emergency, contact local emergency services. For medical, legal, veterinary, border entry, airline, or safety advice, consult the appropriate professional or official authority.
