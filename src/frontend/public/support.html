Help with SproutRoute.

How to reach us, fixes for common problems, and links to our policies.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Contact

Email: nitish.prasad@gmail.com

For fastest triage, include your device model, iOS version, app version, destination prompt if relevant, and a screenshot or short description of what went wrong. Do not send full child names, precise home addresses, medical records, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Common issues

  • Trip generation failed: check your connection, simplify the prompt, and try again. The app uses the live SproutRoute backend for planning.
  • Weather looks different from the generated plan: forecasts can change. iOS may refresh destination forecasts with WeatherKit while the original itinerary was generated from backend planning weather.
  • Local reminders do not appear: confirm notification permission is enabled in iOS Settings.
  • Saved trips disappeared: saved iOS trips are local to the device unless a future sync feature is added. Uninstalling the app can remove local data.
  • Safety or legal guidance seems wrong: treat SproutRoute as informational and verify with official sources before relying on it. Use Report a problem with this plan on the plan to tell us.
  • Sharing a plan with family: share the plan's link from SproutRoute. The people you send it to can open the itinerary, weather and packing list in any web browser; they don't need the app. Anyone who has the link can view the plan, and the link stops working 7 days after the trip ends.
  • Stopping a shared link: open the plan in SproutRoute and choose Stop sharing. On the web, use Stop sharing in the browser where you made the link. If you can't, email us the link and we will stop it.
