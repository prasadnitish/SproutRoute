Privacy Policy

Built for parents. Careful with family data.

SproutRoute helps parents and guardians plan family trips. We collect only the information needed to generate and save trip guidance, operate the service, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests.

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Scope and audience

SproutRoute is a general-audience travel planning service for adults, parents, and guardians. It is not directed to children under 13, and children under 13 should not use SproutRoute directly. Parents may enter a child's age range or travel needs to receive family-appropriate planning suggestions, but they should not enter a child's full name, contact information, school, precise home address, medical records, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Information we collect

  • Trip planning content: destination, origin if provided, travel dates, trip length, family composition, child ages or age ranges, pet type and travel details, pace, interests, accessibility notes, food preferences, and other free-text planning details you choose to provide.
  • Profile import content: travel preferences pasted from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another assistant if you choose to import a reusable profile. The iOS app stores this locally unless you separately use an authenticated web feature.
  • Saved trip data: generated itinerary, weather summary, packing list, safety guidance, saved packing progress, reminders, and local widget snapshots.
  • Device and app data: app version, locale, timezone, unit system, operating system version, error information, diagnostic events, and similar reliability data.
  • Location data: destinations and map queries you enter. If a web or app feature asks for device location and you grant permission, we use it only to help plan or contextualize the trip. You can deny location permission.
  • Support communications: your email address and message contents when you contact us.

How we use information

  • Generate itineraries, packing lists, weather summaries, pet travel checks, and family safety guidance.
  • Save trips and packing progress locally on your device, and support widgets, App Intents, Spotlight, Live Activities, and reminders.
  • Refresh destination weather and detect meaningful forecast changes.
  • Debug crashes, prevent abuse, monitor service reliability, and improve the product.
  • Respond to support, privacy, deletion, and App Store review inquiries.

Third-party services

SproutRoute uses service providers to operate core features. Each provider receives only the information reasonably needed for its task.

  • AI providers: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, and DeepSeek may process trip planning content to parse requests, generate itineraries, summarize safety guidance, or create packing recommendations.
  • Weather providers: Weather.gov and Visual Crossing support web and backend weather planning. On iOS, Apple Weather and WeatherKit may refresh destination forecasts and display Apple Weather attribution where required.
  • Maps, geocoding, and places: OpenStreetMap Nominatim, Google Places, Apple Maps, and MapKit support destination lookup, place enrichment, maps, and handoff to Apple Maps.
  • Safety and advisory data: government travel advisory sources, static car-seat rule datasets, airline pet policy datasets, and pet entry rule datasets support safety cards. Some neighborhood safety functionality may use Amadeus Safe Place where configured.
  • Analytics, hosting, and storage: the web app may use PostHog for product analytics. The current native iOS app does not include the PostHog SDK and does not send iOS app events to PostHog. Supabase, Railway, and Cloudflare may process authenticated profile data, hosting logs, performance data, and security events where configured.
  • Apple services: App Store, TestFlight, App Analytics, WeatherKit, MapKit, UserNotifications, App Intents, ActivityKit, WidgetKit, and Spotlight may process data according to Apple's policies. We are not responsible for data Apple independently collects through Apple frameworks and services.

AI, safety, and legal guidance

SproutRoute uses AI and third-party data to create planning suggestions. AI output can be wrong, incomplete, or outdated. Car-seat guidance, pet travel guidance, travel advisories, weather forecasts, and neighborhood safety information are informational only. They are not legal advice, medical advice, professional safety advice, or a substitute for checking current requirements with local authorities, airlines, accommodations, veterinarians, government agencies, and other official sources.

See our Safety and AI Disclosures for more detail.

Local-first iOS storage

The native iOS app is designed to work local-first and account-free. Saved trips, imported profiles, packing progress, cached weather snapshots, local notification plans, widget snapshots, Spotlight records, and Live Activity payloads are stored on your device or in the app's App Group container. You can delete this local data inside the app from Settings.

Data retention

  • On-device data: remains on your device until you delete it, uninstall the app, or clear the relevant browser/app storage.
  • Trip requests processed by the backend: are processed to generate results and may appear in operational logs or reliability systems for debugging, abuse prevention, security, and service operation.
  • Authenticated web profile data: if you use authenticated profile features, profile data and feedback remain until you delete them or request deletion.
  • Support messages: are retained as needed to respond to the request, maintain records, and comply with legal obligations.

Deletion and privacy choices

You can delete local iOS data in the app. You can also request deletion of server-side personal information, revoke optional permissions, or ask privacy questions by following the instructions on our User Privacy Choices page.

Sale, sharing, and tracking

We do not sell personal information. We do not knowingly share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If our data practices change, we will update this policy and App Store privacy disclosures before shipping the change.

iOS analytics transparency

The current native iOS build does not include PostHog analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs. The iOS app still communicates with the SproutRoute backend to generate trips, enrich places, and refresh safety or packing guidance. Apple may provide App Store, TestFlight, crash, and App Analytics information through Apple's developer tools according to Apple's policies.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect the information we process. No system is perfectly secure, so please avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information into free-text trip prompts or profile imports.

International users

SproutRoute is operated from the United States. If you use it from another country, your information may be processed in the United States or other locations where our service providers operate.

Changes

We may update this policy as SproutRoute evolves. The updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected in App Store privacy information when required.
