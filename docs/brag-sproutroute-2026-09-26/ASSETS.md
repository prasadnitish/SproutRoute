# Asset provenance

- UI screenshots: captured from SproutRoute's actual React frontend using `scripts/capture-portfolio.mjs`. Fictional family and synthetic trip. Captures preserve visible AI/safety caveats. Google Maps, where visible, retains its UI attribution.
- SproutRoute logo: existing `src/frontend/public/logo.svg`.
- Space Grotesk and Inter: Google Fonts, downloaded September 26, 2026. SIL Open Font Licenses are included beside the font files.
- GSAP: local runtime copied from the previous SproutRoute composition, with its embedded vendor license header retained. See https://gsap.com/standard-license/ for the applicable license.
- Music and tap accents: original deterministic instrumental generated for this film by `scripts/create-demo-score.mjs`, 112 BPM, 60 seconds. No third-party samples or voice likeness. The bundled Brag music was not used because its exact redistribution license was not documented.
- Motion reference: HyperFrames Registry `oversized-cursor` and `ui-3d-reveal` informed the cursor glide/tap and screenshot entrance. No unrelated product artwork from those examples appears in the final composition.
- No stock photography, invented app UI, real customer data, or credentials appear in the package.
