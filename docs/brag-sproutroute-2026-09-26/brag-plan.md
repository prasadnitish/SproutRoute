# SproutRoute — Big trips. Little people.

## Creative brief

SproutRoute turns a family's travel idea into an itinerary, packing checklist, weather context, and travel guidance. The memorable claim is the actual interface headline: “Plan a family trip in one prompt.” The strongest visual is the itinerary unfolding into a practical checklist. Show the working product: enter a request, get a plan, explore days, check packing items, open safety.

The requested master is exactly 60 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps. This explicitly overrides Brag's default 15–25 seconds. Use polished/app-store craft with snappy, spunky editing: large confident type, quick camera moves, tactile taps, then readable holds. No narration was requested. Music and restrained UI sounds carry the edit.

Background #F5F7EF; ink #15372B; brand #15803D; sunny accent #FFCA28. Space Grotesk headings and Inter body preserve the app identity. Use actual screenshots of the React app with a fictional family and recorded synthetic model output. Small “Demo trip · edited for pace” labeling identifies the capture. Do not imply opening hours, venue access, safety facts, or production reliability were independently verified.

## Audio

Final audio: an original 112 BPM instrumental and soft tap accents generated by `scripts/create-demo-score.mjs`. The bundled music was excluded because its exact redistribution license was not documented. The score supplies rhythmic motion cues without a waveform visualization; the ending fades over 1.5 seconds. No narration.
